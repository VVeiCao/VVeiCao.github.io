Takeaway

Traditional metrics cannot tell whether the camera follows the new path or the subject stays intact; RetakeBench checks both.

Construction Pipeline

Training-free. Pseudo-4D ground truth from a single web clip.

Metric Protocol

Two questions per video. Did the camera follow the path? Is the subject intact and in place?

Comparison

The correct retake scores best on RetakeBench. Four cases, seven methods.
