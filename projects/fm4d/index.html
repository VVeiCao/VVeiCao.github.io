Can video models re-film a real moment? A real video is filmed by a single camera (blue). We ask three video models to re-film the same moment from a new camera (red). Without a ground-truth video of the new view, which retake is better?

Motivation

Camera redirection takes a monocular source video and replays the same dynamic event along a target camera trajectory.

High CLIP and VBench scores do not imply successful camera redirection. A wheel-legged robot climbs stairs; the requested camera path is a 90° orbit around it. Method A scores higher on nine of the ten CLIP and VBench columns, yet its camera does not reach the target view and its robot collapses into a tangle of legs and wheels; Method B follows the path and keeps the robot intact, as the visual check confirms.

Construction Pipeline

Construction is fully training-free, so the benchmark scales as more source clips are added. Source clips are curated from in-the-wild YouTube videos: category-level queries fetch candidates, which are split into shot-level clips and turned into object-centric crops with Grounded-SAM masks. Each clip's RGB + mask drives a reconstruction stack that recovers geometry-complete foreground 4D point clouds and a ViPE background. Target trajectories then render depth and rough masks; a frozen prompt feeds the Wan generator, and MaskRefine produces the pseudo-GT target mask used for evaluation.

Metric Protocol

Object fidelity uses SAM3 propagation to extract masks from each submitted RGB video and compares them to the dataset target pseudo-GT mask. Camera-pose accuracy reconstructs the camera path from each generated video with a pinned reconstruction stack and compares it to the requested target trajectory. CLIP, FID/FVD, and VBench are reported alongside.

CLIP-T/F/V: text, adjacent-frame, source-video consistency. VBench: SC subject, BG background, TF temporal-flickering, MS motion-smoothness, AQ aesthetic, IQ imaging, OC overall consistency. Visual check: blind check of all outputs (method names hidden); correct, partly correct, wrong. Camera acc.: RotErr (°), TransErr (units of the source reconstruction), and TransErrraw (computed with scale 1), lower is better. Object fid.: D detection rate, R recognition rate. Subject loc.: cIoU mean cMaskIoU on recognized frames, R·cIoU averaged over all frames.
